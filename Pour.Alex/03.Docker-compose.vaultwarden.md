# Project 2: Vaultwarden (Your Private Vault)

Now that you have the Docker engine running, let's look at the ultimate "sovereign data" app. **Vaultwarden** is a private, local version of Bitwarden. It keeps your passwords, notes, and 2FA codes on **your** hard drive - not on a tech company's cloud.

### The "No Door" Security Policy
Before we start, it is important to understand why this is more secure than a standard website:
*  **The Recipe is Local:** Even though you need the internet to download the "ingredients" (the Docker image), once it is cooking on your PC, it stays there.
*  **No Door Available:** Because this server is running inside your home network and we haven't told your router to let anyone in, there is **no door** for a hacker on the internet to even find.
*  **Strictly Offline:** Day-to-day use (searching passwords or getting login codes) happens entirely between your devices and this folder.

---

### S The Vaultwarden "Recipe"

#### Step 1: Prep the Kitchen
Create a dedicated folder for this project: `C:\Docker\vaultwarden`.

#### Step 2: Gather the Ingredients
Open **Notepad**, paste this "Recipe" (the Compose file), and save it as `docker-compose.yml` inside your new `vaultwarden` folder.

```yaml
services:
  vaultwarden:
    image: vaultwarden/server:latest
    container_name: vaultwarden
    restart: always
    environment:
      - SIGNUPS _ALLOWED=true # Set to 'false' after you create your account to lock the vault from any new local signups
    volumes:
      - ./vw-data:/data
    ports:
      - 8081:80
```

#### Step 3: Cook the Project
1.  Open **PowerShell**.
2.  Go to the folder: `cd C:\Docker\vaultwarden`
3.  Pull the ingredients: `docker compose pull`
4.  Start the cooking: `docker compose up -d`

---

### H How to Use It
*  **Access:** Open your browser to `http://localhost:8081`.
*  **Account:** Create your first (and only) account. This is **local to your machine**. 
*  **Locking the Door:** Once you have made your account, edit your recipe file, change `SIGNUPS _ALLOWED=true` to `SIGNUPS _ALLOWED=false`, and run `docker compose up -d` again to restart it. This "updates" the kitchen and ensures that no one else on your Wi-Fi can create an account.
